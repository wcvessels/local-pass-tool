MACOS AUTO-CLEAR

Off for this session.

On macOS, auto-clear is off by default. When off, LocalPass does not remove copied passwords on timeout, Clear, regenerate, or close; they remain until something else replaces the clipboard. If enabled, LocalPass checks before clearing, but macOS cannot make that check atomic, so a copy made by another app during that race may also be cleared. Only passwords copied after enabling are affected. This setting resets off when LocalPass exits.
